## Executive Summary

With a $1,500 founder budget, Planny cannot buy reach on the top-tier ADHD podcasts/YouTube channels at posted rates (Tracy Otsuka ~$1,500-2,500/mid-roll; How to ADHD ~$8K-15K integrated). The asymmetric play is:

1. **Pre-launch beta access + revenue share** to ONE flagship creator (Tracy Otsuka is the highest-leverage target; Sunsama already owns Jessica McCabe).
2. **3-5 micro-creator (10-100K) paid placements** at $150-500 each, optimized for women-coded ADHD audience.
3. **Two-way Apple/Google Calendar sync** shipped before any creator deal — this is the table-stakes proof point.
4. **Focusmate integration** — natural ADHD complement with a public OAuth API (low cost, high narrative value).
5. **CHADD/ADDitude/ADDA editorial submissions** as free awareness layer (3-6 mo lead time).

## Tier 3: Technical Integrations

| # | Partner | What Planny Gains | Effort | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Apple Calendar (EventKit)** | Read access to all calendars; required for iOS app | S (1-2 wks) | **CRITICAL — Month 1** |
| T2 | **Google Calendar API (OAuth)** | True two-way sync (the wedge vs. competitors who only do EventKit one-way) | M (3-4 wks) | **CRITICAL — Month 1** |
| T3 | **Focusmate (public OAuth API)** | "Start a body-doubling session from your Planny block" — natural ADHD complement, dev@focusmate.com is documented contact | S-M (2-3 wks) | **HIGH — Month 2** |
| T4 | **Toggl Track API** | Time tracking on scheduled blocks → "actual vs planned" feedback loop ADHD users crave | M (3-4 wks) | Medium |
| T5 | **Apple HealthKit (sleep/energy)** | Sleep data → energy-aware planning ("don't schedule deep work after a 4-hr night") — speculative but uniquely differentiating | M (3-5 wks) | Medium-High |
| T6 | **Endel** | "Start focus audio with this block" — adjacent. No public API found; would require BD outreach (hello@endel.io). | L (BD-dependent) | Low |
| T7 | **Notion (official API)** | Push schedule to Notion daily; pull task databases. Notion Calendar itself has no separate API — must use the core Notion API. | M (3-4 wks) | Medium (power-user demand) |
| T8 | **Todoist API** | Most-used ADHD task tool; pull tasks into Planny week | M (2-3 wks) | High |
| T9 | **Things 3** | iOS power-user tool, but **NO public API** — only x-callback-url; partner program does not exist publicly. URL-scheme integration only. | S (1 wk, limited scope) | Low |
| T10 | **TickTick API** | Often-overlooked ADHD favorite, has documented API | S-M | Medium |

**Critical EventKit limitation to surface:** Apple EventKit does NOT let third-party apps write to Google/Outlook calendars surfaced through iCloud. To deliver true two-way sync with Google Calendar, Planny MUST integrate directly with Google Calendar API via OAuth — building on EventKit alone yields read-only functionality for non-iCloud sources. This is technically why competitors fall short and why two-way Google sync is a defensible wedge.

## Critical Insights

1. **Tracy Otsuka is the single highest-leverage relationship in the entire ADHD ecosystem for Maya, and she's unclaimed.** Sunsama took Jessica McCabe; Inflow has reputation problems; Shimmer is a coaching not planner play. Whoever locks Tracy in for 2026 owns the women-coded ADHD planner narrative. **A rev-share + co-design pitch (not a sponsorship buy) is the only winning approach at Planny's budget.**

2. **Two-way Google Calendar sync is technically a competitive moat, not a feature.** Apple EventKit's write limitations mean most iOS-native ADHD apps fail at true two-way sync — they're effectively read-only on Google/Outlook. Building direct Google Calendar API OAuth integration is 3-4 weeks of work that creates a durable wedge against EventKit-only competitors. **This must ship before any creator deal.**

3. **The $1,500 budget should NOT be spent on a single big creator.** Better allocation: $800 on Kristen Carder episode (her audience = Maya, price is reachable) + $700 across 2 micro-creators with attribution UTM. This buys 3 distinct touchpoints in a 30-day window for compounding social proof.

4. **Focusmate is the easiest, highest-narrative-value integration win.** Public OAuth API exists, dev@focusmate.com is the documented contact, and "ADHD planner + body doubling" is a story creators will tell for free. Estimated 2-3 weeks of build time.

5. **The ADHD coach ecosystem (ACO/ADDA/PAAC directories) is a sleeping channel.** ~3,000-5,000 credentialed ADHD coaches collectively reach ~500K-1M ADHD adults via mailing lists. A 25% rev-share affiliate program seeded through Dr. Tamara Rosier's network (former ACO president) could deliver more attributable signups in Months 4-9 than any single creator deal. **Dr. Rosier should be courted as an advisory board member, not a sponsored creator.**
