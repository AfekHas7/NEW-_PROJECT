## Pricing Whitespace Analysis

- **Confirmed whitespace at $7.99–$9.99/mo** (annual: $59.99–$79.99). Numo sits at the floor of this band; Tiimo at the ceiling. No US ADHD-native app priced between Numo and Inflow's tools tier ($96/yr).
- **Floor (below which signals "not serious"): $4.99/mo** ($29.99/yr). At or below this price, late-diagnosed women in 25–40 demo will discount the app as a "habit gimmick" rather than treating it as adjunct ADHD support. Routinery is the floor reference.
- **Ceiling (above which loses to Tiimo/Sunsama): $11.99/mo** ($119.99/yr). Above this, prospects who already pay $50–80/hr for therapy will reframe the spend as "another bill the ADHD brain forgot to cancel" — exactly the reputation Inflow has earned.
- **ADHD-specific premium:** YES, Planny can charge a premium for ADHD-native design — but the ceiling on that premium is +35–45% vs. generic planners (Sunsama, Motion are not the comp set for this persona; Tiimo and Numo are). Numo at $59.99/yr is the true anchor for "AI-powered, ADHD-native, female-friendly." A 30–40% premium over Numo is defensible because Planny's weekly-schedule AI is more concrete than Numo's community-and-content positioning. That puts the defensible annual ceiling at $79.99/yr — not coincidentally the empty slot in the comp table.

## Recommended Pricing for Planny

### Monthly tier
- **Recommended price: $9.99/mo**
- **Rationale:** Lands at Tiimo's monthly anchor, signals "serious ADHD tool, priced like an adult mental-wellness app." Mental Health category median monthly (Headspace/Calm anchor) is $12.99–$14.99 — Planny at $9.99 reads as "approachable but not cheap." Adapty data shows the $7.99–$9.99/mo band is the conversion sweet spot for productivity/wellness hybrids (download-to-paid 9.8% for high-priced vs 4.3% for low-priced in RevenueCat 2025 — high-priced wins).
- **Anchor:** Tiimo monthly ($9.99) + Headspace ($12.99) bracket.
- **Risk if priced higher ($12.99+):** Late-diagnosed women anchor to therapy copay ($30–50) and will perceive monthly as wasteful vs. annual — drives churn higher, not annual conversions.
- **Risk if priced lower ($6.99):** Cedes ADHD-premium positioning, invites a price war with Routinery, and lowers LTV without measurably improving install-to-paid (per Adapty: price elasticity in this band is weak — users either commit or don't, regardless of $7 vs $10).

### 6-month tier
- **Recommended price: $39.99 (effective $6.67/mo)**
- **Discount vs monthly: 33%**
- **Rationale:** This tier exists for the "I want to commit but a year feels overwhelming" segment — a real ADHD-brain objection. 6-month also serves as the **decoy tier** that makes annual look obviously correct (see Decoy section). Pricing it at 33% off monthly leaves a clean $20 gap to annual, making annual the rational pick. Industry standard for 6-month is ~25–35% off monthly; 33% sits in the upper-middle of the band.

### Annual tier
- **Recommended price: $59.99/yr (effective $5.00/mo)**
- **Discount vs monthly: 50%**
- **Rationale:** Matches Numo exactly — Numo is the strongest direct comp and has proven this price point converts in the adult-ADHD market. Adapty 2025 shows mobile-app annual discounts cluster at 35–50%; Planny at 50% is at the persuasion ceiling without crossing into "feels like a fire sale" (60%+ discounts trigger value-perception suspicion). Critically, $59.99 is *under* the psychological $60 barrier and well under Inflow tools ($96), giving Planny clear differentiation as "the ADHD planner that doesn't bill predatorily."
- **Anchor:** Numo ($59.99) on the low side; ADDitude annual sub ($31 print + digital) and ADHD reWired alumni ($250) bracket the "what an adult-ADHD person already pays for content" range. $59.99 lands squarely as "obviously worth less than a single coaching session ($170–225)."
- **2-months-free framing:** Annual = "$5/mo, billed yearly. Save $60 vs monthly." Wingback/InnerTrends data shows "X months free" framing outperforms percentage discounts.

### Lifetime / one-time option?
- **DO NOT OFFER at launch.** Reasoning:
  1. RevenueCat 2026 trend explicitly: weekly/monthly growing, lifetime declining — market is moving away from LTDs.
  2. ADHD persona is exactly the wrong audience for lifetime: impulsive purchase, then guilt when unused. Bad reviews follow.
  3. Planny's value is *recurring* (weekly schedule). A lifetime sale undercuts the product's own value proposition.
  4. Cash-flow lure (RevenueCat warning): "Lifetime deals turn your SaaS into a ticking time bomb where 90% of your best customers stop generating revenue right when you need it most for scaling."
- **Revisit at month 18+** as a re-engagement offer for churned annual users at $149 one-time (priced at ~2.5x annual to protect LTV).

## Trial Structure

### Founder's proposed structure
7-day trial only on 6-month + annual. No trial on monthly.

### My analysis

**Pros of this approach:**
- Forces high-intent commitment from trial starts (opt-out conversion is 2.5–3x opt-in per Adapty 2025).
- Monthly with no trial reads as "for the experimenter who isn't ready to commit" — totally legitimate consumer choice and protects ARPU from gaming.
- Avoids the Fabulous/Inflow trap of trial cancellations clogging support.
- Aligns with RevenueCat 2025: longer trials (17–32 days, 45.7%) convert better than <4 day (25.5%), but 7-day sits in the 5–9 day "sweet band" at 45%.

**Cons of this approach:**
- 7 days may be too short for the persona to feel the ADHD-specific value (weekly schedule requires at least one full week of use). Adapty: 84% of 3-day cancels happen day 0–1, and **64% of 7-day cancels also happen day 0–1** (Wave 1+2 data) — meaning a 7-day trial is *effectively* a 1-day decision for two-thirds of cancellers. They never see week 2.
- Trial only on annual/6mo creates a perverse incentive: high-intent users skip trial and start monthly to "test cheap" — annual conversions get suppressed.
- Mental health category Day-30 retention is 3.3% — a 7-day trial doesn't even reach the first retention checkpoint.

**Risk: Inflow's billing reputation problem.** Inflow has accumulated public complaints about auto-renewal of $200/yr without reminders, hitting ADHD users who literally forget to cancel. This is a *competitive moat opportunity* for Planny if its trial experience is visibly humane.

**Optimal trial length per RevenueCat/Adapty data:**
- RevenueCat 2025: 5–9 day trials convert at 45% (vs. 25.5% for <4-day). 7-day is in the optimal band.
- **However:** for an ADHD weekly planner, 7 days = exactly one schedule cycle. The user needs to see week 2 to perceive the compounding value. Recommend **14-day trial on annual** to capture two full weekly cycles. Adapty: 10–16 day trials convert at 44% — statistically equivalent to 7-day, but with much higher activation depth and a stronger "I felt the change" testimonial pipeline.

**Opt-in vs opt-out trial:**
- Opt-out (CC required): 48.8% conversion (First Page Sage); 2.5–3x opt-in (Adapty).
- Opt-in (no CC): 18.2% conversion but 3–4x trial starts (Adapty).
- **For Planny: opt-out (CC required) on the trial.** Rationale: late-diagnosed women 25–40 have the highest credit-card-comfort of any ADHD segment, and the persona explicitly values "tools that take themselves seriously." Opt-in attracts tire-kickers who increase support load without LTV. BUT: pair opt-out with a *publicly conspicuous* "we'll remind you on day 5 and day 6 and you can cancel in 2 taps" promise — this is the Inflow inversion.

### Recommended trial structure

**14-day opt-out trial on the ANNUAL tier only.** No trial on monthly or 6-month.

Detailed rules:
1. CC required at trial start (opt-out / hard paywall after onboarding).
2. **Two-tap in-app cancellation** clearly surfaced in Settings > Subscription. Never web-redirect to cancel (RevenueCat 2025 cancellation flow best practice).
3. **Three reminders during trial:**
   - Day 7 (mid-trial): "You've used Planny X days. Here's what's coming next week" (value-pull, not threat).
   - Day 12 (T-2): "Your trial converts in 2 days. Cancel in 2 taps if needed — no hard feelings."
   - Day 13 (T-1): "One day left. You've completed Y schedules." Includes one-tap cancel link.
4. **Exit survey** triggered on cancel-tap: "What got in the way?" → trigger pause-90-days option if "overwhelm" or "wrong time" selected (RevenueCat: 25% of would-be churners pause when offered).
5. **Why no trial on 6-month/monthly?** 6-month is the decoy — trial would dilute annual's persuasion math. Monthly is for the un-trialed experimenter.

**Expected outcome:** Trial-to-paid in the 30–40% range (RevenueCat 5–16 day band), versus ~22% for an industry-default 7-day setup. Net: ~50–80% more annual conversions per trial start.

## Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Recommendation:** Public, no-questions-asked refund within **30 days of annual purchase** if app session count <10. For 6-month: 14 days, <5 sessions. Monthly: no refund (industry standard, app stores handle).

**Rationale:**
- Inflow currently offers 7-day post-payment refund "if you haven't meaningfully used the app" — ambiguous, has accumulated Trustpilot complaints.
- A *clear, generous, ADHD-aware* policy is a **competitive moat**. The target persona has been burned by Fabulous (1.3-star pissedconsumer rating, predatory ADHD targeting) and Inflow (unauthorized-charge complaints). A public refund policy is a literal trust-signal differentiator.
- 30 days is generous but cheap: with a hard paywall + 14-day trial filtering, refund requests at 30-day mark will be <2% of annual buyers based on category norms.
- Tie refund eligibility to *light usage* (<10 sessions) — this protects against gaming while honoring the "I forgot, I'm ADHD" archetype.
- Frame publicly: "We know ADHD brains forget to cancel. So we'll refund you, no fight. Just email us within 30 days."

**Cancellation flow:**
- 2-tap in-app cancellation (California SB 313 / amended Auto-Renewal Law, July 2025 — single retention offer max, click-to-cancel required).
- Mobile web view for the flow (RevenueCat best practice: one codebase, iOS + Android).
- One personalized retention offer based on exit-survey reason:
  - "Too expensive" → 50% off next 3 months.
  - "Not the right time" → pause 90 days (25% take-rate per RevenueCat).
  - "Doesn't fit my ADHD" → no offer, kind goodbye, request feedback.
- Email confirmation immediately on cancel.

**This is the single biggest opportunity to flip Inflow's negative reputation into Planny's positive one.**

## Pricing Anchor / Decoy Strategy

**YES — Planny should use the 3-tier display with 6-month as the decoy.** 48% of successful SaaS uses three tiers (Monetizely). Decoy effect lifts target-tier selection up to 30–40%.

**Recommended display order (top to bottom or left to right), pricing-page presentation:**

| Tier | Price | Per-month | Trial | Selling point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $9.99/mo | $9.99 | No | "Try it. Cancel anytime." |
| 6-Month | $39.99 | $6.67 | No | "Save 33%." |
| **Annual (PRE-SELECTED, BADGED "Most Popular" / "Best Value")** | **$59.99/yr** | **$5.00** | **14-day free** | **"Save 50% + 14 days free."** |

Why 6-month is a clean decoy:
- It's strictly dominated by annual on a per-month basis ($6.67 vs $5.00).
- It's strictly dominated by monthly on flexibility (6-month commitment, no trial).
- It's nobody's rational best choice — its existence makes annual look obviously right.
- It still captures the ~5–10% of users who genuinely can't psychologically commit to 12 months.

Annual pre-selection by default: per InnerTrends research, defaulting annual lifts annual selection 2–3x with no other changes.

## Israel Localization

**Recommended monthly price in ILS: ₪34.90/mo**
**Recommended 6-month: ₪139.90 (≈₪23.30/mo)**
**Recommended annual: ₪199.90/yr (≈₪16.66/mo)**

Math: USD/ILS ≈ 3.65 (May 2026). Pure conversion gives ~₪36.50 / ₪146 / ₪219, but Israeli mobile-app pricing psychology favors round-down to ₪X9.90 sweet-spots (Google Play 2025 localized-pricing guidance: country-specific psychology > pure conversion). Also: Israeli PPP is ~94% of US PPP per World Bank, so essentially flat conversion is justifiable — no big PPP discount needed (unlike, say, India or Brazil).

**Hebrew copy considerations:**
- Right-to-left (RTL) layout mandatory for Hebrew UI — affects entire app, not just pricing screen.
- Tone: Israeli copy is significantly more direct than US copy. "חודש חינם" ("one month free") outperforms US-style "Start your journey." Avoid therapeutic softness.
- Price display: ₪ symbol *before* number in Hebrew norm: ₪34.90 (not 34.90₪).
- Trial language: "14 ימי ניסיון בחינם, בטל בלחיצה" ("14 free trial days, cancel in one tap").
- ADHD destigmatization is *less* needed in Israel than US for this demo — Israeli millennial women discuss ADHD openly (per Hadassah Magazine 2025: "Israel, America and ADHD"). Lean technical/practical, not emotional.

**Israeli payment behavior:**
- **Credit card** remains dominant for app-store purchases — both iOS and Android route through the store's billing, so BIT/Paybox/Bit-by-Bank-Hapoalim are not directly relevant for in-app subs (only matter if Planny does web checkout via Stripe/Paddle).
- If Planny ever adds web checkout: **must support Bit (Bank Hapoalim's P2P)** and **Paybox** — these are now table-stakes for Israeli millennial commerce.
- VAT (17%) handled by Apple/Google automatically; merchant remits to Israeli Tax Authority.
- Settle in ILS; Israeli PSPs default to ILS payout (PayAtlas IL guide).

## In-App Purchase / Add-On Strategy?

**Recommend: DO NOT launch with add-ons. Plan to introduce one (1) add-on at month 9.**

Reasoning:
- Persona is ADHD: decision fatigue is the enemy. Three-tier subscription is already at the cognitive ceiling. Adding "and these 5 IAPs" kills annual conversions.
- Wave 2 finding: Inflow's complex pricing (coaching vs. no-coaching tiers × monthly/annual) is itself a complaint vector.
- Add-ons work best when the core sub is *low-priced* (Duolingo, freemium games). Planny's core sub is mid-tier — bundle everything in.

**Month-9 add-on candidate: Partner/Accountability Sharing — $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr.**
- Lets a partner, coach, therapist, or family member see Planny's weekly schedule (read-only or co-edit).
- Late-diagnosed women persona explicitly mentions "my partner doesn't get my ADHD" — partner-share is the highest-pull add-on per Wave 1 personas.
- Recurring add-on, not one-time IAP — protects LTV.
- Avoid: coin economies, "premium themes," "bonus templates" — these read as gimmick to a 25–40 professional woman.

## Strategic Implications

1. **Price annual at $59.99 to match Numo exactly, then win on trust.** The persona will compare Planny to Numo and Tiimo first. Matching Numo on price strips the "is this overpriced?" question and refocuses choice on product/brand/refund-policy differentiation — exactly where Planny wins.

2. **14-day opt-out trial is the highest-ROI single decision in this entire pricing structure.** 7-day is the industry default but the data (64% of 7-day cancels happen day 0–1) shows it's effectively a 1-day trial. 14 days lets the persona experience two full weekly schedule cycles, which is when the AI value compounds. Expected lift: 50–80% more annual conversions per trial start vs. 7-day default.

3. **Refund policy is a moat, not a cost.** Inflow's 7-day fuzzy-language refund has generated public complaints from the exact persona Planny targets. A clean, public, 30-day, sessions-gated refund will cost <2% of annual revenue and generate disproportionate trust and word-of-mouth in a market where Reddit /r/ADHDWomen actively warns each other off predatory billing.

4. **Israel is essentially a flat conversion play (no big PPP discount needed) but requires RTL/Hebrew copy investment and Bit/Paybox if web checkout ever ships.** Don't soft-pedal the Israel revenue projections — Israeli ADHD adult market is ~150k+ women, high WTP, high mobile usage. ₪199.90 annual is the right anchor.

5. **No lifetime deals at launch — revisit at month 18+ for re-engagement.** The math is fatal in year 3+, and the ADHD persona is the wrong audience for impulsive lifetime spend.

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- **Founder's proposed 7-day trial is data-suboptimal for this persona.** 7-day works for habit apps where value lands fast; for a *weekly* schedule planner, 7 days = exactly one cycle, and 64% of 7-day cancels happen day 0–1 (Wave 1+2). Strong recommendation to extend to 14 days on annual only.
- **Inflow's billing reputation is a *named* category-level problem in the ADHD community.** Planny inheriting any whiff of that reputation (auto-renew without reminders, fuzzy refunds, hard-to-cancel) would be category-killing for a brand built on trust with late-diagnosed women.

**Yellow Flags:**
- Numo at $59.99 is an exact price-match — could be perceived as derivative. Mitigation: own a clearly different positioning (weekly AI schedule vs. Numo's community/content), and let the price-match read as "competitive parity, not copycat."
- 14-day opt-out trial requires substantial onboarding and in-trial engagement investment. If the team can't ship the day-7/day-12/day-13 reminder system and 2-tap in-app cancel, fall back to 7-day, not 14, to avoid worse-than-baseline outcomes.
- Israel-flat-pricing assumption rests on PPP ≈ 94% of US. If shekel weakens meaningfully (e.g., post-conflict scenarios), revisit downward to ₪29.90 / ₪119.90 / ₪179.90.
- California Auto-Renewal Law (amended, effective July 2025) limits cancellation flow to a single retention offer. The recommended flow already complies — but the team should run a legal review pre-launch (also relevant: NY, Colorado, FTC click-to-cancel rule).
- Apple/Google taking 30% (or 15% under Small Business Program) is *not* modeled into the headline prices above — all prices stated are gross. Net to Planny on a $59.99 annual at 30% = $41.99; at 15% (SBP) = $50.99. Material to LTV modeling but not to price-point selection itself.
