## US Market (Primary)

### Market Size & Distribution

- **Total US adults with ADHD:** ~15.5M (6.0% of adults; ~1-in-16) — CDC NCHS, MMWR Oct-Nov 2023 survey, published 2024
- **Late-diagnosed adults share:** Over half of current adult diagnoses were first made in adulthood; 61% of women with ADHD were diagnosed as adults vs 40% of men (CDC MMWR; ADDitude summary)
- **Implied late-diagnosed adult women TAM:** If women are ~50% of the 15.5M (≈7.75M) and 61% are late-diagnosed → **~4.7M US late-diagnosed adult women** (best estimate, derived from CDC ratios)
- **Age 25-40 slice (Maya beachhead):** Roughly 35-40% of adult ADHD population falls in this band → **~1.6-1.9M US women 25-40, late-diagnosed** as the addressable beachhead pool

**State-level prevalence (children data — best available proxy; CDC does not publish state-by-state adult ADHD):**

| Tier | States | Childhood ADHD ever-diagnosed |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| Highest | Louisiana (16.5%), Rhode Island (15.9%), South Carolina (15.8%), Maine (15.5%), West Virginia (15.1%) | South + parts of New England |
| High-mid | Pennsylvania (7.0% current dx — Northeast high), Kentucky, Mississippi, Alabama, Tennessee | Appalachia + South |
| Low | California (5.6%), Oregon (5.1%), Washington, New York, Hawaii (8.3% — outlier high in West), Massachusetts (5.0%) | West Coast + parts of NE |

Source: CDC State-based Prevalence of ADHD Diagnosis and Treatment 2016-2019 and 2020-2023; Statista compilation; CDC MMWR.

**Key insight:** Highest *childhood* diagnosis rates are in South + Appalachia (lower-SES, higher Medicaid screening). But *Maya* (educated late-diagnosed woman 25-40) is concentrated in different geographies — see below.

### Late-Diagnosis Women Density Indicators (Maya Geography)

Childhood prevalence is a poor proxy for the late-diagnosed-adult-woman beachhead because:
1. Maya was *missed* as a child (internalizing/inattentive presentation)
2. She self-identified via social media or therapy as an adult
3. She skews urban, college-educated, knowledge-worker

**Better proxies — where Maya concentrates:**

- **TikTok #ADHDinWomen / #ADHDwomen geographic skew:** Top creator audiences (Tracy Otsuka, Jessica McCabe/HowToADHD, KC Davis) skew toward US coastal metros + Texas urban triangle (Harvard Dash study; GMA reporting)
- **ADDitude readership:** 2.5M monthly users, 675K newsletter subs; print subs concentrate in NY/NJ/CA/MA/IL based on US ADHD-media patterns (ADDitude About page; Nieman Lab)
- **Telehealth ADHD clinic geography (Done, Cerebral, Klarity, Circle):** Heavily marketed in CA, NY, TX, FL, WA, IL, GA, CO — these were the states where ADHD stimulant dispensing rose fastest 2019-2021 (California study, NCBI PMC10948562)
- **Self-diagnosis-to-clinical-intake funnel:** Seattle psychologist reported TikTok drives ~50% of ADHD intake requests (2023); Seattle, Portland, Austin, Brooklyn, LA, DC are documented hotspots

**Maya density ranking (composite proxy, Medium confidence):**

1. **Greater NYC + NJ + Hudson Valley** — highest ADDitude readership density + therapy market
2. **California Bay Area + LA + San Diego** — telehealth-saturated, knowledge worker
3. **Pacific Northwest (Seattle + Portland)** — late-dx-positive culture, TikTok hotspot
4. **Texas urban triangle (Austin, Dallas, Houston)** — fast-growing ADHD telehealth market
5. **Boston + Cambridge** — academic + healthcare workforce, lower prevalence but high diagnostic literacy
6. **DC + Northern Virginia** — knowledge-worker dense
7. **Denver + Boulder** — wellness culture overlap
8. **Atlanta** — Southeast hub
9. **Chicago** — Midwest hub
10. **Minneapolis-St. Paul** — surprisingly strong ADHD-women community presence

### Beachhead Recommendation

**Recommended first sub-market:** **Greater NYC + Brooklyn + NJ knowledge-worker women 25-40 who follow ADDitude / Tracy Otsuka / HowToADHD**

**Why:**
- **Density:** Highest concentration of ADDitude print/digital subs + ADHD therapists per capita
- **Receptiveness:** Late-diagnosis is culturally normalized in NYC women's media (NYMag, Cup of Jo, Romper coverage 2023-2025)
- **Accessible channels:** TikTok ADHD creators with NYC audiences (Sarah Pikus, Stef Reinhart, others) charge $500-2000/post — within $1,500 budget for one micro-influencer collab
- **Payment friction:** Apple Pay + card universal; no localization required
- **Time zone:** ET aligns founder support hours; Israeli founder can cover ET evenings

**What winning here proves:**
- That late-diagnosed women 25-40 will pay $9.99-14.99/mo for an ADHD-specific weekly planner over Sunsama/Motion/Akiflow
- That TikTok organic + micro-influencer drives <$50 CAC
- That weekly retention >60% W4 is achievable on the Maya persona
- Geographic concentration enables word-of-mouth and creator content loops

**Expansion path:**
NYC/NJ Maya → West Coast Maya (LA + Seattle + Bay Area) → Texas urban triangle + Atlanta → broader US ADHD women → US ADHD men + non-ADHD-adjacent (executive function struggles) → Israel pilot → English-speaking int'l (UK/CA/AU) → EU

### Specific Tactics for US Launch

- **App Store storefront optimization:** US storefront primary; keywords "ADHD planner," "ADHD weekly schedule," "ADHD women," "late diagnosed ADHD," "executive function planner"
- **Time zones for support:** ET (9am-9pm) as primary; founder in Israel can cover ET evenings = IL late afternoon/evening; add PT support 9-5 via async/Intercom for West Coast scaling
- **Payment methods:** Apple Pay (iOS in-app purchase), Stripe for web upgrade flow, Google Pay for Android later. No special integration needed.
- **Pricing:** $9.99/mo, $79.99/yr per Wave 3 C4 (positioned below Motion $19, above Notion AI $10)

## Israel Market (Secondary)

### Market Size & Adoption

- **Israeli adults with ADHD (best estimate):** ~388K adults — derived from 4% adult prevalence × ~9.7M population × ~64% adult share. Estimate range 350-450K. Source: Springer health policy paper (Cost effectiveness of optimal treatment of ADHD in Israel, 2019) which uses 4% adult estimate projected from childhood data.
- **Israeli children ADHD diagnosis rate:** ~14% of ages 5-18 (2016 report cited in Hadassah Magazine 2025) — among highest in OECD
- **Late-diagnosis adult women slice (estimate):** If Israel mirrors US 61% female late-dx ratio → ~120-140K Israeli adult women late-diagnosed (Low confidence)
- **Trend:** Hadassah Magazine March 2025 reports adult ADHD diagnoses trending up sharply in Israel; behavioral therapy in Israel has been "tweaked to accommodate the growing number of adult patients" who arrive more impaired

### ADHD Awareness Trend in Israel

- Adult ADHD increasingly recognized; Adina Maeir (Hebrew U-Hadassah Occupational Therapy) developed leading cognitive-function intervention
- Israel actively researching ADHD epidemiology and treatment; siblings-of-ADHD study by adhdevidence.org population-wide
- Late-diagnosis women movement lags US by ~3-5 years but trending — Hadassah Magazine 2025 piece signals mainstream attention
- Hebrew-language ADHD content on social media is sparse vs English — opportunity for first-mover ADHD app with Hebrew UI

### Israeli Mobile Productivity App Market

- **Middle East productivity/utility apps:** +491% YoY paid installs on Android, +99% on iOS (martechvibe / Intelligent CIO Middle East, April 2026) — strong subscription growth signal
- **Israeli smartphone penetration:** Among world's highest; digitally-savvy population (Expert Market Research)
- **Willingness-to-pay caveat:** Global research shows higher WTP for entertainment/video vs productivity apps; productivity remains a tougher monetization category but iOS users in MENA over-index on WTP (Intelligent CIO Middle East)
- **iOS share in Israel:** ~55-60% (above global avg), favorable for Apple-first launch

### Hebrew RTL Localization

**Engineering effort estimate (solo founder, SwiftUI/React Native assumed):**

| Task | Effort |
|------|--------|
| RTL layout mirroring (Auto Layout + leading/trailing fixes) | 1-2 weeks |
| Date/time/calendar localization (Gregorian + Hebrew calendar optional) | 3-5 days |
| Number/currency formatting (₪, ILS) | 1 day |
| String externalization + key audit | 3-5 days |
| Icon/animation direction audits | 2-3 days |
| QA pass with native Hebrew speaker | 1 week |
| **Total** | **3-5 weeks if RTL built-in from day 1; 8-12 weeks retrofit** |

**Translation cost estimate:**
- ~3,000-5,000 source words for a planner MVP UI + onboarding + paywall + error messages
- Professional EN→HE rates: $0.10-0.18/word for app localization (Translated, Ulatus, Asialocalize)
- **Total translation cost: $300-900** for initial; +$150-300/yr for ongoing string updates
- Add native Hebrew copy editor: $200-400 for tone polish (ADHD-friendly Hebrew is a niche skill)
- **Recommendation:** Use Lokalise or Crowdin ($50-100/mo) + freelance Israeli ADHD-aware translator (₪150-200/hr)

**Cultural adaptations needed:**
- **Weekend = Friday-Saturday**, not Sat-Sun. Week starts Sunday. This is the *single biggest* product change — affects every weekly view, recurring schedule logic, "weekend" labels, planning rhythm
- **Israeli holidays calendar:** Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot, Passover, Independence Day, Shavuot, plus Tisha B'Av — must integrate via Hebrew calendar API or hardcode lookahead
- **Shabbat-aware scheduling:** Notification suppression Fri sundown → Sat sundown (opt-in)
- **Hebrew calendar dual-display:** Optional, but appreciated by religious users
- **RTL-first UI:** Hebrew speakers expect proper mirroring; half-baked RTL is widely mocked in IL App Store reviews
- **Voice & tone:** Hebrew ADHD content tends to be warmer/more direct than English — avoid overly clinical translation
- **Military service context:** Many young Israeli adults have ADHD diagnosis history from IDF mental health screening — leverage this familiarity

### Regulatory Cost

**Privacy Amendment 13 (effective Aug 14, 2025):**
- **ADHD = Information of Special Sensitivity (ISS)** under Israeli law (health/mental health data); triggers heightened obligations
- **DPO mandatory when:** Database holds ISS on >100K individuals; OR processing involves systematic monitoring
- **Planny scenario:** 10K threshold mentioned in brief likely refers to the registration/notification trigger; the 100K threshold is the firm DPO appointment trigger under Amendment 13 text (verify with Israeli counsel — search results show 100K for ISS notification of DPO identity to PPA)
- **Non-compliance fines:** ₪2/individual standard; **₪4/individual for ISS** databases — at 10K Israeli users with ADHD data, exposure is ₪40K (~$11K); at 100K, ~$110K
- **Conservative interpretation:** Treat ADHD data as ISS from day 1, even sub-100K

**Expected timeline to threshold:**
- 10K Israeli users: 12-18 months post-Hebrew launch (assuming 0.5-1% penetration of late-dx adult ADHD women in IL)
- 100K Israeli users: unlikely within 36 months as solo founder

**DPO cost (Israel):**
- Fractional/external DPO retainer: ₪3,000-8,000/mo (~$800-2,200/mo)
- Full-time in-house: ₪25,000-40,000/mo (~$7K-11K) — not viable solo budget
- **Recommendation:** Fractional DPO via firm like APM, Pearl Cohen, or Naschitz Brandes when crossing ~5K IL users; budget ~$1K/mo from month 12

**Other Amendment 13 obligations from day 1:**
- Hebrew-language privacy notice (granular, explicit consent for ISS)
- Data flow mapping documentation
- Breach notification readiness (72-hour rule, GDPR-aligned)
- Right to access/delete/correct (consumer rights mirroring GDPR)

## Market Entry Risks

### US Risks
- **TikTok ADHD content backlash:** Petrie-Flom Center (Harvard 2025) flagged ADHD misinformation on TikTok; <50% of top 100 ADHD TikToks align with DSM-5. Risk: regulatory scrutiny on apps marketed via TikTok claims; mitigate by clinical-tone marketing, no diagnostic claims
- **CDC data revision risk:** Adult ADHD prevalence climbed sharply 2019→2023; possible mean-reversion if telehealth scripts tighten (DEA controlled-substance telehealth rule still uncertain)
- **Competitive saturation in NYC:** Sunsama, Motion, Akiflow heavily market to knowledge workers in NYC — Planny must own "ADHD" wedge, not generic productivity
- **CAC inflation on TikTok:** Mental-health app advertisers (BetterHelp, Cerebral) bid up TikTok CPMs; organic creator route is more durable
- **Apple in-app review:** Mental health classification may trigger longer review; do not claim "treats ADHD" or "diagnoses" — positioning as "planner for adults with ADHD"

### Israel Risks
- **Small market math:** ~120-140K Maya-equivalent IL women × 1-2% conversion × $40-50 ARPU = **$50K-140K ARR ceiling**. Israel cannot be a primary market — only a strategic + emotional anchor
- **Hebrew RTL engineering load:** 3-5 weeks of dev time = ~10-15% of founder year-1 capacity; opportunity cost is US feature velocity
- **Amendment 13 compliance distraction:** ISS classification of ADHD data creates legal overhead even sub-10K users (consent UX, breach docs, Hebrew privacy notice translation)
- **Payment fragmentation:** No Bit/Paybox recurring billing means subset of Israeli users will not subscribe (some prefer Bit over credit card); estimate 5-10% revenue drag vs hypothetical Bit support
- **Hebrew content gap = double-edged:** First-mover ADHD Hebrew content is opportunity, but also means founder must produce or fund Hebrew creator content from scratch (no influencer ecosystem to plug into)
- **Weekend convention:** If product ships Sat-Sun weekend by default for IL users, immediate uninstall risk — must be Sun-start-of-week and Fri-Sat weekend from day 1 of Hebrew launch
- **Reservist/war disruption:** Solo Israeli founder reservist call-ups (ongoing 2024-2026) materially impact velocity; mitigate via async-friendly tooling and 1-2 freelance contractors on retainer

## Critical Insights

1. **NYC + NJ is the highest-density Maya market, NOT Louisiana or South Carolina.** Childhood ADHD prevalence (which is highest in the South) is misleading for the late-diagnosed adult-woman beachhead. Maya concentrates where ADDitude readership, telehealth ADHD scripts, and TikTok ADHD content audiences overlap — that's NYC, LA, Seattle, Austin, Boston, DC.

2. **Israel cannot exceed ~$140K ARR even at saturation.** Israel is a strategic anchor (founder home, Hebrew first-mover, Apple Tier 1, emotional/credibility play), not a revenue play. Plan it as a $50K-100K ARR Phase-3 addition, not a primary market.

3. **Bit/Paybox are a marketing dead-end for SaaS subscriptions.** They do not support recurring billing. Israeli launches must lean on Apple IAP + Google Play. Do not chase Bit integration — it's technically infeasible for subs.

4. **Amendment 13's ISS classification is the binding Israeli constraint, not Hebrew translation.** Translation is $300-900 one-time. The recurring overhead is consent UX, Hebrew privacy notice, breach readiness, and fractional DPO at scale. Budget $1K/mo DPO retainer starting month 12.

5. **Weekend = Friday-Saturday is the make-or-break Hebrew product change.** A weekly planner that ships Sat-Sun weekend logic to Israeli users will get 1-star App Store reviews within 48 hours. Sun-Thu workweek + Fri-Sat weekend + Hebrew calendar holidays must ship in v1 of Hebrew app.

## Data Gaps

- **No state-by-state adult ADHD prevalence data exists publicly** — CDC publishes child-only state breakdowns. Adult state estimates would require BRFSS custom analysis or commercial dataset (e.g., IQVIA, Trilliant Health).
- **No direct adult ADHD prevalence survey in Israel since ~2019.** The 4% figure is a projection. Could be higher (closer to 6% US rate) or lower.
- **No published ADDitude subscriber-by-state data.** Inferred from coverage focus + general ADHD-media patterns.
- **TikTok #ADHDinWomen audience geography is proprietary.** TikTok For Business or Tracy Otsuka's media kit could provide; not in public web.
- **Hebrew ADHD-women creator landscape is undocumented in English sources** — requires Hebrew-language search (founder advantage).
- **Israeli HMO digital health referral pathway** is poorly documented in English; would need direct conversations with Clalit/Maccabi digital health teams.
- **Amendment 13's exact DPO threshold for non-ISS-only databases** is interpreted differently across legal commentaries (10K vs 100K); needs Israeli counsel clarification before scaling.

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- **Bit/Paybox cannot do recurring subscriptions** — any GTM messaging promising "pay with Bit" is technically false. Hard constraint on Israeli payment UX.
- **ADHD data = ISS under Amendment 13** — Israeli launch requires day-1 Hebrew privacy notice + explicit granular consent + breach plan. Cannot ship Hebrew MVP without these.
- **Weekend = Sat-Sun in unmodified Hebrew app would tank App Store reviews instantly** — must ship Sun-start week + Fri-Sat weekend in v1 Hebrew.
- **Israeli market revenue ceiling (~$140K ARR)** is below what some founders assume — do not over-invest in IL at expense of US scaling.
- **TikTok ADHD content scrutiny rising (Harvard Petrie-Flom 2025)** — marketing claims need clinical accuracy; do not claim diagnosis or treatment.

**Yellow Flags:**
- Adult ADHD prevalence rates climbed sharply 2019-2023 (15.5M); possible regression if DEA telehealth controlled-substance rules tighten further in 2026-2027 — could reduce diagnosis pipeline and TAM growth.
- Israeli reservist call-ups (ongoing) impact solo founder velocity; Israel-focused work should be back-loaded to Phase 3 (months 12-24) when product is more mature.
- 4% Israeli adult ADHD estimate is a 2019-era projection from childhood rates; real adult dx rate in 2026 could be 30-50% higher given the awareness trend Hadassah Magazine 2025 flagged — upside if true.
- Highest childhood-ADHD-prevalence states (LA, SC, MS, AL, KY) are NOT the Maya beachhead but DO represent a long-tail ADHD market for Phase 2 expansion via different channels (Facebook groups, Christian wellness, value pricing).
- Apple App Store review for mental-health-adjacent apps in 2025-2026 has lengthened (3-7 days vs 1-2 historically); plan IL Hebrew app review buffer accordingly.
- DPO cost ($800-2,200/mo fractional) is meaningful overhead — sustained ARR from IL must exceed this before scaling IL marketing aggressively.
