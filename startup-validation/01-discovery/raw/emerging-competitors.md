# Emerging & Stealth Competitors — Planny

**Phase:** Phase 3 — Wave 2 (B4 — Deep tier addition)
**Project:** startup-validation (Planny)
**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Confidence:** Medium-Low — by definition lower because these are smaller/newer entities with thinner public signal. Where claims rely on a single source, that is flagged inline.

---

## Executive Snapshot

Round 1 surfaced eight named recently-launched or recently-active apps that overlap meaningfully with Planny's positioning. Round 2 surfaced one accelerator-backed assessment-tier player (Neurotype, Techstars Spring 2026) and one TestFlight beta (MyADHD). Round 3 revealed that the indie/build-in-public layer for ADHD productivity is shallow but active. Round 4 confirmed that VC dollars are flowing to ADHD-adjacent care (coaching, medtech, assessment) rather than to direct planner competitors — which is good news for Planny's specific lane in the short term, but a leading indicator that capital will rotate into the planner layer next.

The single highest-priority threat is **Saner.AI**: it is the most-cited "ADHD AI assistant" in 2026 ranking content, is backed by Techstars + Google Accelerator, and explicitly markets the same "AI that understands what you meant to write" capture-and-schedule wedge Planny is targeting.

## Recently Launched (last ~12 months)

### 1. Flint ADHD Daily Planner
- **Launch:** May 2026 (App Store) — fresh, ~weeks old as of report date
- **Founder:** James R.C. Smith — marketing professional, father, ADHD self-identified
- **Location:** Not disclosed in source (likely UK based on naming/spelling conventions; unconfirmed)
- **Product:** Daily planner with three signature features: (1) stoplight check-in (green/yellow/red capacity rating that re-shapes the day), (2) voice capture that auto-sets priority + time, (3) AI extraction of tasks from brain-dump notes
- **Target user:** Adult ADHD, capacity-variable users — strong overlap with Planny's late-diagnosed-women persona although gender isn't explicitly targeted
- **Pricing:** $5.99 one-time on App Store (iOS 16+, iPhone/iPad/Mac/Vision)
- **Traction:** Unknown — too new to read App Store reviews reliably
- **Funding:** None disclosed; appears solo-founder bootstrapped
- **vs. Planny:** Direct overlap on the "voice/brain-dump → AI structured plan" wedge. Differentiator for Flint = stoplight capacity check-in (a strong ADHD-native concept Planny should evaluate). Pricing is one-time vs. Planny's presumed subscription — could be a competitive pressure point.
- **Threat level:** **MEDIUM-HIGH** — same wedge, very recent, single-founder so easy to under-estimate but also easy to out-execute on multi-platform + women-specific branding.

### 2. Saner.AI
- **Launch:** Active and gaining ranking visibility through 2025-2026; not new but accelerating
- **Founders:** Not surfaced in search; YC/Techstars-affiliated team
- **Location:** Not surfaced
- **Product:** "AI personal assistant for ADHD" — unified notes + email + calendar + chat-driven task management. Marketed as "your Jarvis."
- **Target user:** ADHD knowledge workers and entrepreneurs (broader than Planny's persona, but Planny's persona is a subset)
- **Pricing:** Not surfaced in this pass
- **Traction:** Ranked #1 in "best ADHD apps 2026" by multiple SEO-leaderboard articles (Saner.AI's own blog, OnePageCRM, others). Heavy content marketing presence — they are clearly the loudest voice in this space right now.
- **Funding:** Seed; investors include **Value Create Ventures, Techstars, Google Accelerator**. Crunchbase shows ~$120K disclosed (likely undercounts non-disclosed checks). [Source: PitchBook, Crunchbase, Tracxn]
- **vs. Planny:** Closest direct competitor in the "AI-native, ADHD-first scheduling" lane. Saner is broader (notes + email + calendar) — Planny can win by being narrower and more ritual/weekly-cadence focused.
- **Threat level:** **HIGH** — best-resourced, best-positioned, fastest-moving direct competitor that did NOT appear in the original Wave 1 roster. **Founder should treat this as a Wave 1 entry, not an "emerging" one.**

### 3. Lately
- **Launch:** Early April 2025 (App Store), TechCrunch coverage April 26, 2025
- **Founder:** Erik MacInnis — indie solo developer
- **Location:** Not disclosed (Canada per Startup Ecosystem Canada coverage)
- **Product:** Gamified time-management app — countdown notifications (30/10/5 min before departure), Live Activities on iPhone/Watch, point-reward system with four difficulty tiers ("Goldfish," "Coffee," "Grown Up," "Yoda")
- **Target user:** ADHD adults with time-blindness/punctuality challenges — narrow but very tight persona fit
- **Pricing:** Free + Premium at $3/mo or $10/yr
- **Traction:** TechCrunch feature is meaningful PR for a solo indie; downloads not disclosed
- **Funding:** Indie / bootstrapped
- **vs. Planny:** Adjacent, not direct — Lately is point-solution (be-on-time), Planny is weekly-scheduling-system. Could be a complementary tool or could expand toward Planny's lane.
- **Threat level:** **LOW-MEDIUM** — narrow today, but the gamification + Live Activities pattern is a strong UX move and a real risk if MacInnis extends scope.

### 8. Indy (by Shimmer)

- **Threat level:** **HIGH** — well-funded parent, free distribution, defensible cross-sell economics. Planny needs a clear answer for "why pay when Indy is free."

## Implications for Planny

### Most concerning emerging threats (ranked)

1. **Saner.AI — HIGH.** This should have been in Wave 1. Best-funded, best-positioned, best-marketed direct competitor. Their breadth (notes + email + calendar + chat) is also their weakness — Planny can win on focus + ritual + women-specific persona.

2. **Indy (Shimmer) — HIGH.** Free distribution backed by a $3.5M-funded coaching business that uses the planner as a top-of-funnel. The threat is *economic*, not product: Planny will have to defend a pricing premium against a free, well-funded alternative.

3. **Flint — MEDIUM-HIGH.** Launched ~weeks before this report. Same brain-dump-to-AI-plan wedge. Stoplight check-in is a strong native ADHD UX concept Planny should evaluate adopting or differentiating against.

4. **MyADHD (TestFlight) — MEDIUM.** Feature set is the closest 1:1 match to Planny on paper. Unknown funding/team — Planny founder should enroll in the TestFlight personally.

5. **neurolist — MEDIUM.** Cross-platform shipped product with the "magic subtasks" + voice-playlist UX. Feature-complete enough to be a real comparison point in App Store reviews.

### Signals to watch over the next 6 months
- **Saner.AI raise.** They are positioned for a $5-15M Series Seed in next 6-12 months. When that closes, expect a marketing surge.
- **Indy public launch.** Watch Shimmer's social channels — Indy moving from creator-beta to App Store is the moment Planny's free-tier strategy needs to be locked in.
- **Flint v2 / pricing change.** $5.99 one-time pricing is unsustainable for a single-founder. If Smith moves to subscription or raises, threat level steps up.
- **YC W26 demo day (~August 2026) and S26 demo day (~December 2026).** Watch for any direct ADHD-planner entrant.
- **Techstars Spring 2026 demo day (~Q3 2026).** Watch Neurotype's positioning — if they extend down-funnel toward patients, they become competitive.
- **ADHD Flow State Spring 2026 cohort.** Request alumni list; competitor candidates will emerge in 3-6 months.
- **Khosla / PsyMed second bets.** Both have one ADHD investment (Neurode). A second bet from either signals thesis hardening.
- **Worklife Ventures.** Brianne Kimmel doing a second ADHD investment after Shimmer would signal the productivity-VC layer entering.

### Should Planny accelerate launch timeline?

**Yes — moderately.** The 12-18 month window assumption from Wave 1/B1 is intact but trending tighter on the *AI-scheduling-for-ADHD* lane specifically:
- Saner.AI is **already there** with the AI-native positioning.
- Flint launched ~weeks ago with the same wedge.
- Indy is coming with free distribution.

Recommendation: tighten launch window to **9-12 months**, prioritize the late-diagnosed-women-25-40 persona positioning (which no competitor in this report owns explicitly — that is Planny's defensible wedge), and consider an early waitlist + community-building motion in the next 60 days to lock in mindshare before Saner's likely Series Seed marketing surge.

## Flags

### Red Flags
- **Saner.AI is the dog that wasn't in the Wave 1 roster.** This is a process flag, not just a competitive one — if Saner was missed, what else was missed? Recommend a Wave 1 audit before B5.
- **Indy (Shimmer) introduces a "free, well-funded" competitor.** Pricing strategy must answer this BEFORE Planny launches paid tiers publicly. If Planny launches at $9.99/mo and Indy launches free with coaching upsell, conversion will be brutal.
- **Capital is hardening around ADHD-tech.** $2.7B in mental-health digital funding in 2024 (+38% YoY) + named active investors (Worklife, Khosla, Techstars, YC, Masawa) = the 12-18 month window is real but actively closing.

### Yellow Flags
- **Solo-founder indie launches are clustering** (Flint, Lately, NoPlex all 2025-2026, all solo, all ADHD-personal-story-led). Individually low-threat; collectively they fragment App Store search results and crowd the category.
- **Techstars made two ADHD-adjacent bets in two years** (Saner.AI, Neurotype). Pattern, not anomaly.
- **The "stoplight capacity check-in" (Flint) and "magic subtasks + voice playlist" (neurolist) are both strong ADHD-native UX innovations Planny does not currently have.** Feature-gap risk.
- **Open-source ADHD tooling (ADHDExec, Leantime) exists but is shallow.** If a well-funded company forks Leantime + adds a polished mobile front-end, that's a fast path to a credible competitor.
- **No competitor in this report explicitly owns the "late-diagnosed women 25-40" persona.** This is Planny's defensible wedge. Move fast to claim it in brand and content before someone else does.
- **ADHD Flow State Accelerator** is a future-competitor generator the founder should monitor every cohort.
