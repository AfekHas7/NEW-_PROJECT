- **Founders:** Helene Lassen Nørlem & Melissa Würtz Azari (Azari is herself neurodivergent) (T2 ArcticToday, 2024)

**Product:**
- Visual, icon-based daily timeline planner; designed for neurodivergent users
- Co-planner (AI) — voice or text dump, AI breaks into achievable tasks (T2 howtogeek, 2025)
- Calendar import from Apple/Google/Outlook (**one-way only** — no two-way sync) (T1 Tiimo FAQ)
- Profile sharing — up to 5 profiles under one subscription (family plan)
- Platforms: iOS, watchOS, Web — Android is in beta/limited
- Won **iPhone App of the Year 2025** (T2 Daring Fireball/Apple, Dec 2025)

**Pricing (US, 2025-2026):**
- iOS: $12/mo or $54/yr ($4.50/mo) — alternate tier $79.99/yr (T2 multiple, 2025)
- Web: $10/mo or $42/yr ($3.50/mo)
- Family: $119.99/yr (5 profiles)
- 7-day free trial; no permanent free tier

**Market Position:**
- Tagline: "Visual Planner for Every Neurotype" / "ADHD-Friendly Planner"
- Target: neurodivergent adults (ADHD, autism, AuDHD) — explicit positioning
- Differentiator: visual icon timeline, designed-with-and-for community

**Traction (T1-T2):**
- 3M+ downloads, 1M+ users claimed (Tiimo website, 2025)

Reason: Same persona (ADHD adults), category-defining brand, just won Apple's biggest endorsement, growing AI features, family plan exists. Their weaknesses (no two-way sync, no real collaboration, no partner mode) = Planny's opening.

## Competitive Landscape Summary

**Market concentration:** **Fragmented but bifurcating.**
- ADHD-native tier: Tiimo dominates brand (Apple award), Inflow leads in market share (14%, T3), Numo + Routinery are niche.
- AI-scheduler tier: Motion is the funded leader ($50M ARR, $550M val) and is pivoting B2B; Reclaim is now Dropbox-owned; Akiflow + Sunsama are bootstrapped/lean.
- **No competitor combines AI weekly planning + native ADHD UX + mobile-first + partner/shared mode.**

**Gaps in the market — what no competitor does well:**
1. **Two-way calendar sync + ADHD-friendly visual layer.** Tiimo is one-way; ADHD-aware tools mostly don't write back to Google/Apple Calendar.
2. **Mobile-first weekly planning with constraint-based reschedule.** Motion has the AI, Sunsama has the ritual — neither is mobile-first or ADHD-explicit.
3. **Partner/shared calendar for ADHD adults.** Family plans exist (Tiimo) but no real partner-mode for shared scheduling (e.g., neurotypical partner ↔ ADHD partner co-planning). This is a wide-open whitespace.
4. **Voice-first capture → scheduled plan.** Tiimo's Co-planner is text/voice but young; Routinery has voice guidance during execution. No one combines voice-capture + AI-schedule + ADHD context.
5. **Honest pricing in the $7-10/mo target range with full features.** Tiimo's iOS price is $12/mo; Numo competes here at $7.99 but has bug issues; Routinery is cheaper but doesn't plan weeks.
6. **Apple Calendar parity.** Most AI schedulers (Motion, Reclaim) lean Google Workspace. Apple users (Planny's core mobile persona) are underserved with native two-way sync + AI.

**Positioning opportunities for Planny:**
- "ADHD-native weekly planner that actually owns your calendar" (two-way sync + reschedule)
- "Built for the late-dx adult, mobile-first, partner-ready"
- Price wedge: $7-10/mo undercuts Tiimo iOS ($12), Motion ($19), Sunsama ($25)

**Competitive moat for whoever wins:**
1. Distribution lock-in (Apple endorsement / Google Spark partnership)
2. Calendar trust — once users hand over the calendar with write access, switching cost is real
3. Community + brand voice (Numo's "cringe-free", Tiimo's design)
4. Clinical credibility (Inflow has this — others would need it)

## Strategic Implications for Planny

1. **Tiimo is the existential threat, not Motion.** Motion is pivoting B2B agentic; Apple just crowned Tiimo as the consumer ADHD app. Planny must out-execute Tiimo on its weakest axes: **two-way calendar sync, mobile partner mode, AI reschedule logic.** Don't try to beat them on visual design.

2. **Win the late-dx US woman 25-40 by combining Numo's tone + Inflow's credibility + Sunsama's capacity awareness — minus their bugs/coaching-upsell/price.** None of the three nails all three. Tone matters more than features in this persona's initial purchase decision.

3. **Pricing wedge is real at $7-10/mo.** Tiimo iOS is $12, Motion $19, Sunsama $20+, Inflow $22+, Akiflow $19. Only Routinery ($3.99) and Trevor ($3.99) are cheaper, and they don't plan weeks. **$8.99/mo is defensible and differentiated.**

4. **Two-way Apple Calendar sync is a moat.** Most competitors have Google-first calendar logic. Planny built mobile-first on Apple = native two-way sync is plausible IP/UX advantage, especially pre-WWDC 2026 (Apple Intelligence likely to deepen calendar APIs).

5. **Partner/shared mode is genuinely unclaimed.** Every competitor's "shared" feature is either team-billed (B2B) or profile-sharing (Tiimo family). No one has built a true couple/partner ADHD-coordination mode. **This is the most defensible single feature whitespace.**

6. **Watch the Dropbox/Reclaim mobile launch and Apple Intelligence + Gemini Spark (May/June 2026).** Reclaim getting a mobile app would compress timeline. Apple's Intelligence + scheduling could neutralize Tiimo's Apple advantage and create a new platform layer for everyone.

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- **Motion's $550M val + $50M ARR + B2B pivot** = potential ability to launch a consumer-ADHD play if they choose. Watch for them acquiring an ADHD brand.
- **Tiimo's Apple App of the Year 2025 + AI Co-planner** = brand + product momentum is real, not a paper threat.
- **Reclaim free forever + Dropbox distribution** = if Dropbox launches Reclaim Mobile + adds ADHD framing, Planny faces a free competitor with hundreds-of-millions distribution.
- **Inflow's billing/cancellation complaints** = reputation risk for the whole ADHD-app category; users come into Planny suspicious.

**Yellow Flags:**
- **Apple WWDC 2026 (June 2026, 2 weeks out)** likely deepens Apple Intelligence + Calendar APIs. Could be a platform tailwind OR commoditization risk.
