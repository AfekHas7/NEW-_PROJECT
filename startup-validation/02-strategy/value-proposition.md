## One-Sentence Value Proposition

**English (master):** "The weekly planner built by an ADHD adult for ADHD adults — voice your week in 60 seconds, two-way synced, no streaks, no shame."

**עברית (משני, ל-Israel marketing):** "מתכנן השבוע שנבנה ע"י מבוגר עם ADHD למבוגרים עם ADHD — תקליט את השבוע ב-60 שניות, מסונכרן דו-כיווני, בלי streaks, בלי בושה."

**מבנה לוגי של ה-statement:**
- **"The weekly planner"** — category claim (weekly cadence, לא daily ritual שנשרף ולא single task); ניצול ציר Y בפוזישנינג שאף מתחרה לא תופס נקייה (Sunsama daily, Tiimo visual day, Routinery routines, Motion auto-day).
- **"Built by an ADHD adult for ADHD adults"** — founder authenticity wedge; השאלה הראשונה ב-r/ADHDWomen על כל אפליקציה חדשה היא "is the founder ADHD?".
- **"Voice your week in 60 seconds"** — wedge wow moment + Aha moment כפי שהוגדר ב-target-audience.md; משיב ישירות ל-"washing machine of thoughts" (ADDitude) ול-"setup overwhelm" pain #1.
- **"Two-way synced"** — wedge טכני נגד Tiimo (one-way), Apple Reminders (iCloud fragmentation), Sunsama mobile-read-only; ה-#1 unmet need בקטגוריה.
- **"No streaks, no shame"** — wedge רגשי נגד הקטגוריה כולה; "I felt like the failure" (Medium), "watching a machine reschedule the same task for the fifth time just adds shame" (Motion review).

### Jobs-to-be-Done

#### Functional Jobs (מה היא מנסה להשיג בפועל)

1. **"Render my chaotic week back to me in <90 seconds without setup"** — verbatim aha-moment job; הקריטריון הקיומי. אם לא רואה את השבוע שלה in-app בסשן הראשון, churn risk 70%+ (target-audience.md).
2. **"Re-plan with me when life happens — don't make me rebuild from scratch"** — mid-week reset job. ה-pain ש-Sunsama ו-Tiimo מאלצים על rebuild הוא Planny's wedge.
3. **"Tell me what to do next without making me decide"** — "I don't need another to-do list. I need someone to tell me what to do next" (recurring sentiment, ADDitude + r/ADHDWomen).
4. **"Remember commitments I forget"** — object permanence proxy. "If your calendar app is buried on page three of your phone, you won't check it" (AFFiNE).
5. **"Show me when I have actual capacity vs not"** — energy-aware. "Planning your whole day the night before then waking up and deciding it doesn't fit today's vibe" (viral tweet).
6. **"Coordinate with my partner without the daily 'what are you doing today?' interrogation"** — partner mode job. אמת כ-whitespace ב-customer-voice.md (Why Is It So Hard to Get My ADHD Partner to Use a Shared Digital Calendar).
7. **"Sync with my Google Calendar (work) and Apple Calendar (personal) without manual re-entry"** — two-way calendar sync.
8. **"Capture the thoughts in my head without making me organize them perfectly"** — brain-dump job ללא taxonomy decisions.
9. **"Be on my phone — work fully on mobile, not read-only companion"** — anti-Sunsama job.
10. **"Help me look at next week without the Sunday scaries"** — anxiety-mitigation use case ב-11pm pre-bedtime check.

#### Social Jobs (איך היא רוצה שיתפסו אותה)

1. **"Help me look like I have it together — at work, with my partner, with my kids"** — performance-of-adulthood job.
2. **"Help me explain my brain to my partner without crying"** — relational legitimacy.
3. **"Stop being 'the chaotic one' in my friend group / group chat"** — peer identity.
4. **"Feel like a 'real adult' even though I was diagnosed at 34"** — late-diagnosis identity reconciliation.
5. **"Stop being the one in my marriage who forgets birthdays"** — partner role-trust.
6. **"Have a Sunday where I'm not bracing for Monday"** — visible calmness as social signal (a partner who sees her relaxed = relational gain).

#### Emotional Jobs (איך היא רוצה להרגיש) — **הכי חשובים**

1. **"Make me feel capable, not broken"** — the deepest job. כל copy של Planny אמור לחזק את זה.
2. **"Stop the washing-machine brain at 2am"** — anxiety-reduction. "Women's swirling minds are like a washing machine of thoughts that never stops" (ADDitude).
3. **"Replace the executive function I don't have, without judgment"** — outsource cognition.
4. **"Have a system that doesn't punish me for being inconsistent"** — anti-shame system requirement.
5. **"Remove the shame of having ADHD-tax subscriptions I'm not using"** — financial-ethics emotion.
6. **"Feel safe trying — knowing I can leave without losing money"** — trust pre-condition for download.
7. **"Feel like the tool actually understands my brain"** — recognition / belonging.
8. **"Stop feeling like I'm failing at being an adult"** — most loaded shame surface.
9. **"Less ashamed every time I miss a day"** — recovery emotion.
10. **"Less alone"** — community-by-proxy via the founder voice.

### Pains (כאבים) — ranked frequency × intensity

#### Hair-on-fire pains

1. **Setup overwhelm — "downloaded 30 apps, abandoned 28"** — Extreme frequency × Hair-on-fire intensity. Tiimo דחפה אותה החוצה ב-onboarding מרוב אפשרויות; Notion הותירה אותה מול דף לבן.
   > "When I first got Tiimo I was really overwhelmed by the number of options for activities and things." (JustUseApp Tiimo)
   > "It made managing tasks feel like another task." (NoPlex review)
   > "Endless customizations take too much time to input even something basic." (Write Mind on Tiimo)

2. **Time blindness** — Very high × Hair-on-fire. ה-day disappears, ה-back-to-back מתפספס.
   > "Get absolutely nothing done the entire day for no good reason and then complete 90% of my tasks from 4:51pm to 4:58pm." (viral tweet, HuffPost)
   > "You go down a rabbit hole and completely lose track of time." (ADDA)

3. **Task initiation paralysis** — Very high × Hair-on-fire. "I know exactly what needs to be done but still find myself paralyzed, unable to start" (3rdWaveTherapy). Clinical core symptom.

4. **App fatigue ("app graveyard")** — Extreme × Hair-on-fire emotionally / Medium functionally.
   > "I've tried Everything. Capital E." (productivewithchris)
   > "Worked brilliantly for exactly two weeks before slowly, inevitably, everything fell apart." (Elephant in the Mind)
   > "I felt like the failure." (Medium, 30+ planners)

#### High pains

5. **Subscription guilt / ADHD tax** — High × Medium-High. "ADHD can turn free trials into months of paying for something you don't use, or worse yet, forgetting how to even log in to cancel" (JD Psychotherapy).
   > "I was charged $95 for a yearly subscription and received emails from the co-founder asking me to take down my review." (Inflow review)
   > "Apple refused to refund me after only using the app for less than a week." (Tiimo App Store)

6. **Streaks shame / red-overdue UI** — Medium frequency × High intensity. "Streaks" / "Don't break the chain" סומנו בקטגוריית **words customers HATE** (target-audience.md). Toxic לקהל הזה.

7. **Calendar fragmentation** — High × High. Work-Google + personal-Apple + planner-separate = "I have to enter the same meeting three times." Tiimo one-way sync = #1 documented vulnerability ב-tiimo.nolt.io.

8. **Partner friction** — High × High. "He asks 'what are you doing today?' and I genuinely don't know." Partner-aware mode חסר ברוב המתחרים (Tiimo profile-sharing only, Inflow community-only).

9. **Object permanence — out of sight, out of mind** — Very high × High.
   > "If your planner lives in a drawer, you'll forget it exists." (AFFiNE)
   > "If your calendar app is buried on page three of your phone, you won't check it." (AFFiNE)

#### Medium pains

10. **Inconsistent capacity day-to-day** — High × Medium. "Monday I can do 10 things, Tuesday I can do 0." "Planning your whole day the night before then waking up and deciding it doesn't fit today's vibe" (viral).

11. **Hyperfocus crash** — Moderate × High. "Glancing over at the clock after being so immersed in a project and realizing that hours have flown by." Missed meals, missed pickups, missed dinner.

12. **Notification blindness** — Medium × Medium. Apple Reminders stacked לאוקיינוס; "12+ alarms" ("TAKE MEDS", "LEAVE NOW", "STAND UP") שהם רעש לבן.

13. **Pricing fairness anxiety** — Medium × Medium. "$22.49/mo? Tiimo עולה $12. Motion $34. Sunsama $20." sticker shock + comparison-to-therapy-session ($170-225).

14. **Mobile-second-class-citizen friction** — Medium × Medium. "Achilles' heel for a $20/month app" (Capterra Sunsama).

15. **Shame from late diagnosis / lost years** — Universal background × Medium-High emotional. "Relief mixed with an unexpected grief. Instead of clarity alone, I found myself mourning lost time and opportunities" (Brilla Counseling).

## What Planny Does NOT Promise

To protect FDA / FTC boundary AND Maya trust, Planny carefully **does not say:**

- ❌ "Treats ADHD" / "Reduces ADHD symptoms" — SaMD regulatory line.
- ❌ "Improves focus" / "Increases attention span" — clinical claim.
- ❌ "Replaces therapy / medication / coaching" — position as complement.
- ❌ "10x your productivity" / "Crush your week" — bro-productivity vocabulary that Maya rejects.
- ❌ "Build the habit that changes your life" — habit-shaming, "Don't break the chain" coding.
- ❌ "ADHD-friendly" — saturated cliché the community openly mocks (target-audience.md).
- ❌ "Discipline" / "Just" — loaded shame words.
- ❌ "Neurospicy" — split community sentiment; many cringe.
- ❌ Specific N claims like "I tried 30 apps and abandoned 28" — viral framing, not measured. Use emotional truth (app graveyard) without false-specific number.

Planny **does say:**

- ✅ "Helps you stay organized"
- ✅ "Designed with ADHD UX principles" (not "ADHD-friendly")
- ✅ "Voice → realistic week in 60 seconds"
- ✅ "Works with your brain, not against it" — verbatim community language
- ✅ "Built by an ADHD adult, for ADHD adults"
- ✅ "30-day refund if it doesn't work for you"
- ✅ "Get unstuck. Actually start. Just one thing."
- ✅ "Late-diagnosed? You're not behind. You just got here."
- ✅ "60-70% consistency is the goal — not perfection."

## Flags

**Red Flags:**

- **Apple WWDC June 8, 2026 (~10 days) — Apple Intelligence iOS 27 calendar AI risk.** Post-Mayday Labs acquisition (April 2024), Apple has had 2+ years building the exact feature stack Planny's voice-to-week wedge depends on. If Apple ships heavy AI calendar with voice input + scheduling at WWDC, Pain Reliever #1 (30-second voice start) and Gain Creator #5 (sub-90-second setup) lose their moat overnight. **Mitigation:** war-room prepared with 3 response scenarios (heavy/light/delayed); positioning shift ready to "ADHD-specific layer on top of Apple Intelligence."
- **Setup-time claim ("60 seconds", "90 seconds") is the load-bearing promise.** If actual onboarding ≥ 2 min, the entire value-prop falls apart in trial → Reddit screenshots → brand damage. **Mitigation:** internal SLA = under 75 seconds median onboarding voice-to-first-plan, tested in beta with 20+ Maya-persona users before launch.
- **30-day refund + day-14 pause email cost us money on purpose.** Pricing model assumes ~5-10% refund rate; if 25%+, unit economics break. **Mitigation:** model in `business-model.md` must include this; refund rate is an early-warning metric for product-market fit, not a cost to minimize.
- **Two-way Apple Calendar sync via EventKit is genuinely hard.** Tiimo, Sunsama, Akiflow all attempted and shipped partial. If Planny ships buggy two-way sync, it becomes the #1 vulnerability cited against us on r/ADHDWomen — the exact wedge we're claiming. **Mitigation:** sync quality is the #1 engineering bar; ship one-way write-back to Apple before claiming two-way externally if needed.
- **Saner.AI marketing surge could claim "voice your week" first** if they close seed in Q3 2026 and reposition. **Mitigation:** ship and market voice wedge within 9 months; lock persona to late-dx women publicly so Saner's broader scope can't claim it.
- **"Built by an ADHD adult" claim must be verifiable.** Maya's #1 sniff-test question. If founder is not visibly ADHD (TikTok presence, public diagnosis story, in-app DM responsiveness), the wedge is fraudulent and Reddit will smell it. **Mitigation:** founder commits to build-in-public TikTok ≥ 3x/week minimum and in-app DM SLA of 48hr.
- **Comorbidity gap (89%) creates expectation mismatch.** Users expecting therapy-grade emotional regulation will churn. **Mitigation:** explicit landing-page copy positioning Planny as planner + complement to therapy, not therapy substitute.
- **iOS-only MVP excludes ~40% of US Maya-persona (Android users).** Equity risk + missed TAM. Black/Latina women are over-indexed on Android in some segments. **Mitigation:** Android within 6 months of iOS launch; communicate the timeline publicly to maintain trust.

**Yellow Flags:**

- **"Just one thing" prompt is low-moat differentiator.** Goblin Tools, Inflow Quinn partial. Combine with capacity-aware re-weight to make it stickier.
- **Partner mode (v1.1) is named but not yet shipped.** Risk: Maya churns to Cozi or Tiimo before partner mode ships. **Mitigation:** publish public roadmap with partner mode target date; ship within 6 months of v1.
- **Energy-aware mode overlaps with Sunsama capacity-aware + Flint stoplight check-in.** Not unique. **Mitigation:** combine with 1-tap reshuffle (Sunsama can't do mid-day re-plan; Flint is daily-only) to make distinct.
- **Voice transcription accuracy for non-white-American-English speakers untested.** Aha moment may fail differentially across demographics. **Mitigation:** diverse beta testing; text-fallback always one tap away.
- **30-day refund must be operationally delivered.** Apple/Google IAP refund processing has constraints; if we can't actually refund within 48hr, the brand promise is a lie. **Mitigation:** ops team / founder commits to manually issuing supplementary refunds where Apple/Google delay; never fight a refund request.
- **Kind notes / weekly check-in could feel like manipulation if mistimed.** Risk: triggers shame instead of warmth. **Mitigation:** opt-in only; never daily; tested with persona users for tone before shipping.
- **"60-70% consistency is the goal, not perfection" copy is community-language but unverified per-creator origin.** Use it but don't claim a specific clinician source. **Mitigation:** attribute to "ADHD coaching community language" if pressed, not a specific name.
- **Founder DM channel is a humans-don't-scale risk.** Works at 100 users; breaks at 10,000. **Mitigation:** plan for graduated response model (founder personally responds to first 500 users; transitions to community manager + founder office hours later).
