# Brand Personality — Planny

**Phase:** Phase 5 — Brand (Personality)
**Project:** startup-validation (Planny)
**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Confidence:** Medium-High — ה-archetype, ה-emotional attributes וה-anti-brand behaviors נשענים ישירות על Phase 3 (customer-voice, target-audience) ו-Phase 4 (positioning Onliness Test, banned vocabulary list); הכיוונים הויזואליים הם **המלצות לאימות** מול 90% ה-UI/UX שהמייסד כבר עיצב, לא תכנון מהיסוד. ## תקציר

הזהות של Planny היא **"הצד החם של החכמה"** — חברה ADHD-savvy שיודעת מה היא עושה (Sage knowledge מהמחקר + מהמייסד), אבל מתנהגת כמו מישהי שישבה לידך בבית הקפה ולא כמו מומחית שמכתיבה לך תוכנית. ה-archetype הראשי הוא **Caregiver עם השפעת Sage** — Planny דואג ל-Maya בלי קורבן עצמי (יש מודל מנוי, יש refund, אין pity), ובלי authority חיצוני (Tiimo הוא ה-authority הזה ו-Maya נטשה אותו). ארבעת ה-pillars (Honest, Calm, Real, Capable) מתמפים ישירות לתכונות אמוציונליות שהיא מחפשת אחרי 5-12 כישלונות productivity-app: Honest = trust שלא ייתפס במנוי, Calm = anti-trigger אחרי 89% comorbidity anxiety, Real = "someone like me", Capable = "I can do this week without performing executive function I don't have." הויזואל (sage green + cream + dusty rose, sans-serif humanist, rounded soft, real ADHD women לא stock) מבדל את Planny במרחב בין Sunsama (sophistication ללא ADHD context) ל-Calm (emotional resonance ללא pragmatism). זה בדיוק החלל ש-Maya מחפשת ולא מוצאת.

## Brand Archetype Analysis

לפי מערך 12 ה-Archetypes של Carl Jung / Margaret Mark & Carol Pearson (*The Hero and the Outlaw*, 2001):

### Primary Archetype: **The Caregiver** (with strong Sage influence)

**Why Caregiver:**
- ב-base motivation, Planny רוצה להוריד stress, להגן על המשתמשת מ-overwhelm, ולספק תחושת ביטחון יומיומית. זה תואם בדיוק את ה-Caregiver core motivation ("to help others, to protect from harm").
- Maya חיה בעולם שמרגיש שהיא צריכה "להחזיק את הראש מעל המים" 7 ימים בשבוע — Planny הוא הזרוע שמושטה. ה-pillar **Capable** מתורגם ל-"I've got your week, so you can have your life."
- אבל **Planny לא martyr ולא enabler** — אין self-sacrifice, אין pity. ה-Caregiver של Planny מקבל כסף ($9.99/$39.99/$59.99), פותר את הבעיה, ומכבד autonomy. זה Caregiver עם boundaries — בדיוק כמו ההורה ה-ADHD-savvy שתמיד רצינו.
- ה-pillar **Honest** הוא ה-anti-shadow של Caregiver: ה-shadow side של Caregiver הוא guilt-tripping ו-codependency; Planny חוסם את זה דרך 30-day refund, 2-tap cancel, ו-zero dark patterns.

**Why Sage influence (לא Hero, לא Magician):**
- Planny מבוסס על מומחיות שני סוגים: founder-lived ADHD experience + customer research (Phase 3 verbatim quotes). זה Sage element אותנטי, לא marketing posture.
- אבל Sage לא מתנהג כ-"authority" (זה בדיוק מה ש-Tiimo עושה עם ה-visual schedule "האופטימלי" שלו, ו-Maya נטשה). Planny שואל **"מה אתה רוצה לעשות השבוע?"** ולא **"הנה התוכנית האופטימלית."**
- ה-pillar **Real** הוא מה שמרכך את ה-Sage: ה-knowledge קיים, אבל הוא מועבר ב-ADHD vernacular, לא ב-clinical/expert vocabulary.
- **המתח הקריאטיבי המרכזי:** Caregiver שיש לו Sage knowledge אבל מתנהג כ-friend, לא כ-expert. זה ה-DNA של ה-brand.

### Pillar → Archetype Mapping

| Pillar | Archetype expression | בקופי / בעיצוב |
|--------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Honest** | Caregiver shadow-blocker | 30-day refund, 2-tap cancel, "no takedown emails" (anti-Inflow), founder ADHD disclosure פומבי |
| **Calm** | Caregiver tone + Sage restraint | אין red overdue, אין streaks, microcopy עם soft amber, haptic לא confetti |
| **Real** | Sage humility | "By an ADHD adult, for ADHD adults" — לא "experts" ולא "clinicians" |
| **Capable** | Caregiver outcome promise | "Your week, voiced" — outcome, לא transformation hype |

### Anti-Archetypes (מי Planny לא — ולמה כל אחד יהרוס את ה-trust של Maya)

- ❌ **The Hero** (Motion): "crush your week", "conquer your day", "productivity warrior" — מנגד את ה-ADHD shame היסטוריה של Maya. Hero = הצד השני של "lazy/undisciplined".
- ❌ **The Ruler** (Sunsama "intentional living", "find your flow"): מרמז על דיסציפלינה, על שליטה, על אסתטיקת CEO. Maya מתעבת את ה-vocabulary הזה — Phase 4 ban list מאשר.
- ❌ **The Magician** (Saner.AI "Jarvis ל-ADHD", "AI-powered transformation"): hype-y, מבטיח קסם. Maya skeptical של "AI" buzzword (Phase 4 — "avoid hype").
- ❌ **The Innocent** (Tiimo's playful visual schedule, "everything's beautiful!"): מנגד את הכאב האמיתי של 89% comorbidity. גם נראה ילדותי לאישה בת 34.
- ❌ **The Outlaw** ("break the rules!", "hack your brain"): לא מתאים לכלי שמטרתו order; "hack" הוא בדיוק במילון השנוא של Maya.
- ❌ **The Jester** ("ADHD is a superpower!", neurospicy memes): גם זה ב-banned vocabulary. ל-Maya יש 89% comorbidity — זה לא superpower.
- ❌ **The Lover** (wellness/Instagram aesthetic, Inflow's green/orange): מבלבל את הקטגוריה (planner, לא wellness ritual).

## Emotional Attributes

### What Planny Should Make Maya Feel

#### Primary (must-have, every session)
1. **Relief** — *"Someone finally gets it. I don't have to explain my brain to this app."*
2. **Safe** — *"I won't be punished for missing. No red dot of shame waiting for me."*
3. **Capable** — *"I can actually do this week. The plan is realistic for *my* energy, not someone else's."*
4. **Seen** — *"This was built by someone like me. The founder is one of us."*
5. **Trusting** — *"They won't trap me in a subscription. 30-day refund, 2-tap cancel. Proof, not promises."*

#### Secondary (nice-to-have, builds delight + retention)
6. **Curious** — *"Voice my week? That's new. Let me try."*
7. **Lighthearted** — *"No streaks? Refreshing. I can breathe."*
8. **Hopeful** — *"Maybe this one will stick. Maybe I'm not the problem."*
9. **Quietly proud** — *"I voiced my week in 60 seconds. That was easy. That counts."*

### What Planny Must NEVER Make Maya Feel

- ❌ **Ashamed** (when she misses a day, a week, a month — no broken-streak guilt-trips)
- ❌ **Stupid** (when she struggles with a feature — copy should never say "just" or "simply")
- ❌ **Anxious** (from notifications — no urgency triggers, no count-down clocks)
- ❌ **Trapped** (in subscription — cancel must always be 2 taps from main settings)
- ❌ **Patronized** (by AI suggestions — never "we recommend you...")
- ❌ **Sold-to** (by upsells — annual plan offered once, never re-prompted)
- ❌ **Compared** (to others' productivity — zero leaderboards, zero "users like you completed X")
- ❌ **Performed-at** (no "rate us!" mid-flow, no "share your achievement!" social pressure)
- ❌ **Clinical** (no "ADHD symptom", no "treatment", no medical framing — FDA/FTC SaMD red line + Maya not in patient mode when using a planner)

### The "Therapist Test"

לכל decision של copy או UI: **Would Maya feel comfortable showing this screen to her therapist on Tuesday?**

If yes → ship.
If no (because it would prompt the therapist to ask "is this making you feel worse?") → redesign.

## Visual Direction (Adjectives + References)

> **הערה קריטית:** המייסד כבר עיצב 90% מה-UI/UX. המסמך הזה הוא **כיוון לאימות + פינות חדות לתיקון**, לא תכנון מהיסוד. RECOMMENDED: review session 90 דקות מול המייסד לפני launch כדי לסנכרן את הקיים מול הכיוונים כאן ולסמן deltas.

### Color Palette (recommended direction)

- **Primary:** Soft sage green / muted teal — calm, balanced, not too feminine, not corporate. (לא ה-Inflow green הרווי — גוון יותר עמום, יותר "linen tablecloth", פחות "smoothie bowl Instagram".)
- **Secondary:** Warm cream / off-white — non-clinical, not stark white. רקעים, surfaces.
- **Accent:** Coral / dusty rose — for highlights, completion states, founder voice signature. **NOT for warnings.**
- **Soft state:** Warm amber / honey — for "hmm, something's off" states, replacing red entirely.
- **Avoid:**
  - **Bright red** (shame trigger — Maya's "red dot of shame" experience with Apple Reminders)
  - **Neon yellow / electric orange** (anxiety trigger)
  - **Corporate blue / black** (Motion-coded, "executive assistant" vibe)
  - **Hot pink / bright purple** (Tiimo/Numo Gen Z-coded, infantilizing for 34-year-old)
  - **High-contrast neon gradients** (ChatGPT/Saner AI hype aesthetic)

### Typography

- **Headers:** Geometric humanist sans-serif — **Inter, Söhne, Untitled Sans, GT America**. Avoid display fonts (Playfair, Cormorant) and condensed sans (Bebas Neue, Druk) — both lean too "magazine" and too "fitness app" respectively.
- **Body:** Reading-optimized sans-serif — **Inter** (free, performant) or **system font stack** for native feel. ADHD adults benefit from generous line-height (1.5-1.6) and slightly larger body (16-17px iOS default+).
- **Accent / founder signature:** Subtle hand-script (e.g., **Caveat**, **Homemade Apple**) **only** for "by an ADHD adult" personal signature touches — once per screen max, never in functional copy.
- **Avoid:** All caps (shouty), letter-spacing on body (Sunsama-coded), serif italic for emphasis (clinical).

### Iconography & Shape Language

- **Rounded corners** (16-24px radius on cards, 12px on buttons) — soft, non-clinical
- **Soft drop shadows** (low opacity, large blur) — depth without flatness; counter to the iOS-flat aesthetic that feels sterile
- **Spacious padding** — generous white space; ADHD UX benefits from low visual density
- **Hand-drawn occasional touches** — founder's signature aesthetic, used sparingly (empty states, milestone moments, NOT functional icons)
- **Avoid:** Hyper-geometric (Notion-coded), 3D bevels (skeuomorphic regression), micro-illustrations of brains/lightbulbs (cringe ADHD cliché)

### Photography / Imagery Style

- **Real ADHD women, not stock** — diverse ages 25-40, in natural environments (home offices, kitchens, coffee shops, commute, bed). UGC-feel beats studio.
- **Embrace:** realistic, slightly messy, lived-in spaces; phone-held-in-hand POV; laundry in background; unfiltered light.
- **Avoid:**
  - Corporate stock photo women in suits at standing desks
  - "Perfect productivity" desk setups (clean white, succulent, MacBook, latte art)
  - Sad-stock photo "woman holding head in hands" (depression-coded, clichéd)
  - Cartoon brains with lightning bolts
  - Diverse-cast stock that's obviously stock-cast diverse

### Motion / Microinteraction

- **Pace:** slow-to-medium ease curves; nothing snappy or aggressive
- **Haptics over confetti** — soft single haptic on completion, not animated explosions
- **Reduced motion default-friendly** — full functionality with iOS Reduce Motion enabled
- **Avoid:** Bouncing, spinning, parallax-heavy scrolls — all anxiety-amplifiers for sensory-sensitive users

### References (apps with vibes we want)

- **Headspace** — calm, accessible, friend-tone. (But ADHD-tuned, not zen-philosophical.)
- **Day One** — personal, journal-like intimacy; type-forward without being cold
- **Linear** (productivity B2B) — clean, minimal, restraint with personality
- **Sunsama** — sophistication of layout (without the aspirational-CEO vibe we're avoiding)
- **Calm** — emotional resonance + trust signals (without the wellness-industry tone)
- **The New Yorker** (digital) — typographic restraint + occasional whimsy (the hand-drawn signature mode)
- **Marco Pierre (Substack newsletter design generally)** — personal essay aesthetic, founder-voice authenticity

### References (apps we want to *avoid* visually)

- **Tiimo** — too playful, too visual-rich, kindergarten-y; the Apple Award is for visual schedule, not for our use case
- **Motion** — too corporate, performance-coded blue+black, executive aesthetic
- **Inflow** — wellness-brand green/orange that screams Instagram ad; coaching-app vibe
- **Apple Reminders** — too austere, no warmth, system-default sterile
- **Notion** — too "everything to everyone", blank-page paralysis aesthetic
- **Routinery / Numo** — too gamified, too bright, Gen Z-coded
- **Saner.AI** — tech-blue, AI-forward, "Jarvis" vibe

## Brand Voice in Visual Elements

(זה ה-bridge בין ToV ל-UI states. ה-tone-of-voice.md המלא יפרט בנפרד.)

### Loading States
- **Avoid:** Bouncing dots ("processing..."), spinning wheels, progress bars מטרה לא ברורה
- **Embrace:** Subtle pulsing dot + microcopy אנושי: *"Thinking with you…"* / *"Listening to your week…"* / *"Putting this together…"*

### Empty States
- **Avoid:** *"No tasks today!"* with a sparkle icon, *"Time to be productive!"*, blank-canvas paralysis
- **Embrace:** *"Today is open. Plan it, or rest. Both are valid."* / *"Nothing here yet. Voice when ready."*

### Error States
- **Avoid:** Red banner with X icon, *"Error!"*, *"Something went wrong"*, technical codes
- **Embrace:** Soft amber tone + *"Hmm, that didn't catch. Try again?"* + retry button. Error as conversation pause, not as system failure.

### Notification Copy
- **Avoid:** *"You've been gone 3 days!"* / *"Don't break your streak!"* / *"X tasks waiting!"* / red badges
- **Embrace:** *"Whenever you're ready — your week is here."* / *"Sunday voice session?"* (opt-in only, never streak-based)

### Confetti / Rewards
- **Avoid:** Animated confetti, badge unlocks, leaderboards, sparkles
- **Embrace:** Subtle haptic + soft chime when user completes voice brain-dump (one-time per session, not gamified, not stored as a "score")

### Onboarding / First-Run
- **Avoid:** 8-screen tour, feature parade, *"Welcome! Let's get started!"* enthusiasm
- **Embrace:** *"Hi. Want to voice your week now, or look around first?"* — choice + agency from screen 1

## Mascot / Symbol Consideration

**המלצה ברורה:** **אסור mascot animated / character-led.** Tiimo, Routinery, Numo נכשלים בזה — קצת ילדותי, מנגד את ה-Caregiver+Sage tone, וגם יוצר UX overhead (character states, voice, personality maintenance).

### הצעות לכיוון icon (Adjectives + Directions, not specs)

ה-skill הזה לא ממליץ icon ספציפי בלי mockups. המייסד יבחר.

## Naming Considerations

**שם:** **Planny** — מאושר ע"י המייסד. הניתוח כאן הוא retroactive validation + flags.

**ניתוח:**
- ✅ **Memorable** — קל לזכור, חמוד, sticky
- ✅ **Pronounceable in English + Hebrew** — לוקאליזציה חלקה (חשוב למייסד ישראלי-אמריקאי)
- ✅ **App Store discoverable** — "ADHD planner" + "Planny" — מתחבר; subtitle יישא את "ADHD-native"
- ✅ **Wordmark-friendly** — אותיות עגולות, "p" כפול ב-"nn" מאפשר typographic play
- ⚠️ **Risk:** דומה ל-**Planoly** (Instagram scheduling tool, well-established) — לא בלבול JTBD ישיר (Planoly = social media) אבל קרוב ב-domain "planner" + שמות מתחילים ב-Plan. **Action:** trademark search ב-USPTO + WIPO; domain check planny.app + planny.com + planny.ai.
- ⚠️ **Risk:** "Planny" יכול להישמע ילדותי / מקטין (-y suffix) — תלוי בעיצוב הויזואלי. **Mitigation:** Sage+Caregiver visual (לא Innocent), typography מבוגרת (לא Comic Sans-vibe), avoid pink/yellow palette.
- ⚠️ **Risk:** Voice-search recognition — "Planny" עלול להתבלבל עם "Plenty" ב-Siri / Google Assistant. **Action:** בדיקת voice-search baseline לפני launch.
- ✅ **Cross-cultural:** אין משמעות שלילית ידועה ברוסית, ספרדית, גרמנית, ערבית (בדיקה ראשונית — מומלץ deep linguistic check לפני expansion ל-UK/EU).

### Tagline candidates (ל-marketing)

1. **"Your week, voiced."** — short, feature-led, memorable, מאומת ב-positioning.md
2. **"By an ADHD adult, for ADHD adults."** — founder credibility, identity-affirmation
3. **"Plan less. Live more."** — outcome-led (a bit clichéd, רך מדי)
4. **"No streaks. No shame. Just your week."** — manifesto-style, חזק לאמין על copy האפליקציה
5. **"60 seconds. Your whole week."** — speed promise, מאומת מ-Phase 4
6. **"The planner that doesn't punish you."** — anti-shame, direct
7. **"Voice it. Plan it. Live it."** — triplet structure, rhythm

**המלצה:**
- **Primary tagline (App Store, hero):** **#1 — "Your week, voiced."** (קצר, ייחודי, מסמל wedge)
- **Secondary tagline (credibility contexts — podcast ads, ADDitude, App Store description):** **#2 — "By an ADHD adult, for ADHD adults."**
- **Manifesto/about page:** **#4 — "No streaks. No shame. Just your week."**

## Anti-Brand Behaviors

מה Planny לעולם לא יעשה (גם אם זה משפר metrics בטווח קצר):

- ❌ **Push notifications מבוססות-streaks** — *"You've been gone 3 days!"* / *"Don't break your streak!"*
- ❌ **Dark patterns בביטול** — auto-renew warnings מטעים, hidden cancel, confirm-shame ("Are you sure? You'll lose progress!")
- ❌ **"Compare with friends" / leaderboards** — Maya doesn't want to compete with her friends on productivity
- ❌ **Gamification מבוססת badges** — לא XP, לא levels, לא achievements stored as score
- ❌ **Sales-y emails** — *"Last chance!"*, *"24 hours only!"*, *"Don't miss out!"*

## Decision Frame for Visual / Brand Choices

When the team designs anything new, ask these 5 questions in order:

1. **Does it shame the user when they fail?** → If yes, redesign.
2. **Is it instantly recognizable as Planny in a sea of productivity apps?** → If no, sharpen identity.
3. **Would Maya feel comfortable showing this screen to her therapist on Tuesday?** → If no, soften.
4. **Does it elevate emotion (anxiety, excitement, urgency)?** → Avoid; emotional register should be calm + warm.
5. **Could a competitor (Tiimo / Sunsama / Saner) ship this exact design tomorrow without changing anything?** → If yes, sharpen the ADHD-native + founder-voice angle.

**Bonus filter for copy specifically:**
6. **Is any word from the Phase 4 banned vocabulary list in this string?** ("ADHD-friendly", "hack", "10x", "crush", "just", "discipline", "streak", "lazy", "neurospicy") → If yes, rewrite.

## Flags

### Red Flags

- **Founder owns 90% of UI already; this doc may conflict with existing visual decisions.** **RECOMMENDED:** 90-minute review session with founder before launch — walk through current UI screen-by-screen against this doc's color/typography/iconography guidance and document deltas. Some current decisions may already align; some may need conscious deviation justification. **Do not impose this doc retroactively without that session.**

- **MVV doc and Tone-of-Voice doc don't exist yet** in `/03-brand/`. This Brand Personality doc is built on user-supplied pillars (Honest / Calm / Real / Capable) + Phase 4 positioning. **If MVV is later authored with different pillars or different mission framing, re-sync this doc immediately.** Pillar drift = brand drift.

- **"Caregiver" archetype risks being interpreted as "soft"** in pitch contexts (investors, App Store editorial, press). Must be balanced by visible **Sage** influence in copy + the founder's lived authority. **If brand reads as only-soft, Sam (men) expansion in v1.2+ becomes harder, and investor narrative weakens.** Pair every Caregiver-leaning surface with one Sage-leaning credibility signal (founder credential, research evidence, refund proof).

### Yellow Flags

- **"Planny" naming has slight collision risk with Planoly** (Instagram scheduling tool, established brand). Different JTBD but shared "Plan" + similar -ly construction. **Action:** USPTO trademark search + domain availability check (planny.app, planny.com, planny.ai) **before any external launch announcement.**

- **Sage green palette + dusty rose may be perceived as "feminine-coded."** This is **by design for beachhead (Maya)** but flag for Sam expansion (months 9+) — may require accent-color variant or expanded palette without core re-brand. **Don't pre-emptively neutralize for Sam now — that dilutes Maya signal which is the wedge.**

- **"-y" suffix in "Planny" risks infantilizing.** Mitigated by Sage+Caregiver visual (not Innocent) + grown-up typography. **Watch for App Store review feedback specifically calling out "feels childish"; if appears 2+ times, treat as signal not noise.**

- **Voice-search recognition risk** — "Planny" vs "Plenty" in Siri / Google Assistant / Alexa. **Action:** Test before launch; if confusion >10%, consider phonetic distinguisher in voice-onboarding ("Planny — P, L, A, double N, Y").

- **The "Therapist Test" framing assumes Maya has a therapist.** ~73% comorbidity with anxiety means most do, but not all. Test still works as design heuristic regardless of literal therapist relationship — frame internally as "would Maya feel proud or ashamed to show this to someone who cares about her wellbeing?"

- **Hand-script accent font for founder-signature touches** carries cringe risk if overused. **Hard rule:** once per screen max, never in functional copy, never for headers. If unsure → don't use.

- **Real ADHD women photography** is high-effort sourcing (vs stock). For v1 launch, **acceptable to use illustration-led** approach instead until UGC pipeline + paid model-shoots are in budget. **Don't use stock as fallback** — illustration > stock when authentic photo isn't possible.

- **"Caregiver + Sage" combination is right for Maya but may need to shift to "Sage + Caregiver"** (Sage primary) if persona analytics show Maya converting better on credibility-led messaging vs emotion-led. **Monitor in first 90 days post-launch.**
